Physics Research & Computing

ANTARES Processing Pipeline

A processing pipeline that converts ANTARES neutrino telescope data (AntDSTs) into a Swim-compatible format. It handles file merging, applies oscillation weights and corrections, and integrates ML-based NNFit reconstructions for analysis.

Tau Appearance Measurement

This analysis investigates tau neutrino appearance from atmospheric neutrino oscillations using ANTARES. It evaluates reconstruction algorithms, implements quality cuts, and uses a profile likelihood approach to assess sensitivity to the νμ→ντ oscillation channel.

KM3NeT/ORCA Reconstruction Studies

An investigation into energy reconstruction biases for muon neutrinos in the ORCA6 detector. Identified track geometry relative to the sparse detector array as the primary cause of systematic energy miscalibrations.

AcubeSAT Mission

A comprehensive radiation study for the AcubeSAT 3U CubeSat. Quantified flux, Total Ionizing Dose (TID), and shielding effectiveness for critical subsystems to guarantee mission success in Low Earth Orbit.

Pulsar Star Classification
